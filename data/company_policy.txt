COMPANY POLICY

1. Vacation Time
Employees receive 20 annual vacation days per year. Time off must be requested at least 2 weeks in advance. Unused vacation days do not roll over to the next year.

2. Remote Work
Remote work is available to eligible employees up to 2 days per week. Fully remote work is not permitted without special authorization from HR.

3. Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 10 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

4. Expense Reimbursement
All business expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the receipt date. Meals during business travel are capped at $50 per day.

5. Office Hours
Core office hours are from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM local time. All employees must be available during this time for meetings and collaboration.
